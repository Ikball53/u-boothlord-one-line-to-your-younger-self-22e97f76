# One Line to Your Younger Self

> The girl you spent a year too scared to talk to marries the guy who just says hi.

**81 characters** — one line, in English, no second sentence.

## Why it should win

- **Told to a younger self, not to nobody.** The "you" is the younger self, and the line reports the ending of a story only the older self has lived to see.
- **Only the older self could say it.** A motto anyone could wear would be "be brave" or "take the chance". This one names a specific wasted year and a specific outcome — knowledge that only exists after the fact.
- **One breath, real voice.** No explaining, no moral, no second sentence doing the work. It reads like something actually said, not written for a poster.
- **The turn stays after one reading.** The twist is in the last words: the winner isn't richer, funnier, or braver — he just said hi. The whole lesson is inside that surprise.
- **Honest enough to sting.** It doesn't comfort the younger self; it tells him the cost of a year of fear, plainly and warmly, without sentimentality.
